PROYEK V4 - SORTING CONVEYOR TIGA JALUR
PLC: Schneider Modicon M221 TM221CE24R

TUJUAN
- Klasifikasi produk A, B, atau reject pada tepi naik sensor masuk.
- Conveyor berhenti saat pusher/gate aktif lalu melanjutkan ke sensor keluar.
- Timeout sequence, bin-full handling, dan counter per kategori.

ALAMAT INPUT
%I0.0  START_PB
%I0.1  STOP_OK
%I0.2  ESTOP_OK
%I0.3  OVERLOAD_OK
%I0.4  ENTRY_SENSOR
%I0.5  TYPE_A_SENSOR
%I0.6  TYPE_B_SENSOR
%I0.7  PUSHER_HOME
%I0.8  EXIT_SENSOR
%I0.9  BIN_A_AVAILABLE
%I0.10 BIN_B_AVAILABLE
%I0.11 REJECT_BIN_AVAILABLE
%I0.12 RESET_PB

ALAMAT OUTPUT
%Q0.0  MOTOR_CONVEYOR
%Q0.1  PUSHER_A
%Q0.2  PUSHER_B
%Q0.3  REJECT_GATE
%Q0.4  LAMP_RUN
%Q0.5  LAMP_BUSY
%Q0.6  LAMP_FAULT
%Q0.7  BUZZER

MEMORY BIT / HMI
%M0    AUTO_MODE
%M1    START_HMI
%M2    STOP_HMI
%M3    AUTO_ENABLE
%M10   RUN_MEMORY
%M11   SAFETY_OK
%M20   PRODUCT_A_LATCH
%M21   PRODUCT_B_LATCH
%M22   PRODUCT_REJECT_LATCH
%M23   SORT_BUSY
%M24   TRANSPORT_DONE
%M25   PUSH_ACTIVE
%M26   PUSH_DONE
%M27   EXIT_PULSE
%M30   JAM_ALARM_LATCH
%M31   BIN_ALARM_LATCH
%M32   ALARM_ACTIVE
%M40   RESET_HMI
%M41   RESET_COUNTERS_HMI
%M42   RESET_REQUEST

PARAMETER SOFTWARE OBJECT
%TM0 TRANSPORT_TIMER        Type=TON, Time Base=100 ms, Preset=20 (2.0 s)
%TM1 PUSH_TIMER             Type=TON, Time Base=100 ms, Preset=7  (0.7 s)
%TM2 SEQUENCE_TIMEOUT       Type=TON, Time Base=100 ms, Preset=80 (8.0 s)
%C0  COUNT_A                Preset sesuai target produksi
%C1  COUNT_B                Preset sesuai target produksi
%C2  COUNT_REJECT           Preset sesuai target produksi

(* NETWORK 1 - safety *)
LD     %I0.1
AND    %I0.2
AND    %I0.3
ANDN   %M2
ST     %M11

(* NETWORK 2 - run latch hanya mode auto *)
LD     %I0.0
OR     %M1
OR     %M10
AND    %M0
AND    %M3
AND    %M11
ST     %M10

(* NETWORK 3 - reset request *)
LD     %I0.12
OR     %M40
ST     %M42

(* NETWORK 4 - tangkap produk A *)
LDR    %I0.4
AND    %M10
ANDN   %M23
AND    %I0.5
AND    %I0.9
S      %M20

(* NETWORK 5 - tangkap produk B *)
LDR    %I0.4
AND    %M10
ANDN   %M23
ANDN   %I0.5
AND    %I0.6
AND    %I0.10
S      %M21

(* NETWORK 6 - selain A/B yang tersedia diarahkan reject *)
LDR    %I0.4
AND    %M10
ANDN   %M23
ANDN   %M20
ANDN   %M21
AND    %I0.11
S      %M22

(* NETWORK 7 - reject bin penuh saat perlu reject *)
LDR    %I0.4
AND    %M10
ANDN   %M23
ANDN   %M20
ANDN   %M21
ANDN   %I0.11
S      %M31

(* NETWORK 8 - mulai sequence jika salah satu klasifikasi berhasil *)
LD     %M20
OR     %M21
OR     %M22
S      %M23

(* NETWORK 9 - transport ke aktuator *)
BLK    %TM0
LD     %M23
ANDN   %M24
IN
END_BLK

(* NETWORK 10 - tandai posisi aktuator dan mulai pusher *)
LD     %TM0.Q
AND    %I0.7
S      %M24

LD     %TM0.Q
AND    %I0.7
S      %M25

(* NETWORK 11 - durasi pusher/gate *)
BLK    %TM1
LD     %M25
IN
END_BLK

(* NETWORK 12 - akhir pusher *)
LD     %TM1.Q
ST     %M26

LD     %M26
R      %M25

(* NETWORK 13 - timeout seluruh sequence sampai exit *)
BLK    %TM2
LD     %M23
IN
END_BLK

LD     %TM2.Q
S      %M30

(* NETWORK 14 - pulse sensor keluar *)
LDR    %I0.8
AND    %M23
ST     %M27

(* NETWORK 15 - counter A *)
BLK    %C0
LD     %M27
AND    %M20
CU
LD     %M41
R
END_BLK

(* NETWORK 16 - counter B *)
BLK    %C1
LD     %M27
AND    %M21
CU
LD     %M41
R
END_BLK

(* NETWORK 17 - counter reject *)
BLK    %C2
LD     %M27
AND    %M22
CU
LD     %M41
R
END_BLK

(* NETWORK 18 - bersihkan sequence setelah exit *)
LD     %M27
R      %M25

LD     %M27
R      %M24

LD     %M27
R      %M23

LD     %M27
R      %M20

LD     %M27
R      %M21

LD     %M27
R      %M22

(* NETWORK 19 - reset alarm setelah penyebab hilang *)
LD     %M42
ANDN   %I0.4
AND    %I0.7
R      %M30

LD     %M42
AND    %I0.11
R      %M31

(* NETWORK 20 - alarm umum *)
LD     %M30
OR     %M31
ORN    %I0.2
ORN    %I0.3
ST     %M32

(* NETWORK 21 - motor berhenti selama push atau alarm *)
LD     %M10
AND    %M11
ANDN   %M25
ANDN   %M32
ST     %Q0.0

(* NETWORK 22 - aktuator hanya satu sesuai latch *)
LD     %M25
AND    %M20
AND    %M11
ST     %Q0.1

LD     %M25
AND    %M21
AND    %M11
ST     %Q0.2

LD     %M25
AND    %M22
AND    %M11
ST     %Q0.3

(* NETWORK 23 - indikator *)
LD     %M10
ANDN   %M32
ST     %Q0.4

LD     %M23
ST     %Q0.5

LD     %M32
ST     %Q0.6

LD     %M32
ST     %Q0.7

UJI PENERIMAAN
1. Simulasikan A, B, dan reject; pastikan hanya satu latch/aktuator aktif.
2. Pastikan produk kedua diabaikan ketika SORT_BUSY=1.
3. Pastikan motor berhenti selama PUSH_ACTIVE dan hidup lagi sesudahnya.
4. Sensor EXIT harus menambah counter yang tepat dan membersihkan sequence.
5. Hilangkan EXIT; setelah 8 s JAM_ALARM_LATCH harus aktif.
6. Penuhkan reject bin dan kirim produk reject; sistem harus alarm tanpa aktuasi.
